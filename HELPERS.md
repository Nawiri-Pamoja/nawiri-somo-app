# For helpers (Jokony) – how you can make this app right

This app teaches children to read and write and to speak English, through Dholuo. The Dholuo in it was written
by a computer program, not by a Luo speaker. Some of it is wrong or sounds strange. **You are the ones who can fix it.**

## Open the helper area
Tap **Helpers / Jokony** on the first screen, then answer the small sum (it only keeps small children out).

## 1. Your name and agreement
Write your name (it goes into the thank-you list in the app) and tick the agreement:
your corrections, words and voices are published freely in the app under the licence CC BY-SA 4.0.

## 2. Check the Dholuo
Tap **Check the Dholuo**. For each entry you see the English and the Dholuo.
- **Correct** – it is right and natural.
- **Fix it** – write the Dholuo the way people really say it in your area. You can add a note (dialect, other word).
- **Skip** – if you are not sure.

Entries the author was unsure about come first. Children see your version at once.

## 3. Record your voice
Tap **Record Dholuo voices**.
- Start with **Sounds** (a, e, i, o, u, ma, me, mi …). Children learn to read with these, so they matter most.
- Then **Words**, **Phrases**, **Sentences**.
- Say each one clearly, once, not too fast, in a quiet place. Listen, then **Keep** or **Again**.

## 4. Add your own words
**Add our own word** – a word from your village, a food, a game, a place. It appears as “Our own words”.

## 5. Send your work
Tap **Export everything**. This makes one file with everything from this tablet.
Send it by WhatsApp or e-mail to the Nawiri Pamoja team. It will be put into the app for all children,
everywhere. Another tablet can also open the file with **Import**.

Erokamano ahinya!
